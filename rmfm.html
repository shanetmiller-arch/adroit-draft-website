Risk Management Framework

A government-grade risk assessment, audit trail, and compliance reporting platform — built to make oversight evidence-based rather than opinion-based.

RMFM is ADROIT's flagship response to recurring risk-management gaps observed across live government programs. Every capability shipped is anchored to operational proof from a real engagement, not a hypothetical use case.

Phase 1 · Scaffold Status: Active Maturity: Developing Category: Risk Management

Core Capabilities

RMFM ships these capabilities in the current draft. Each one maps to a real program need captured during ADEPT consulting engagements.

Risk Assessment

Structured risk identification, scoring, and treatment workflows — aligned to NIST RMF and program-specific control catalogues.

Audit Trails

Tamper-evident change history for every risk decision, with role attribution and full chain-of-custody for downstream audit.

Compliance Reporting

Pre-built report templates for the artefacts oversight bodies actually ask for, with scheduled delivery and export.

Real-Time Monitoring

Live dashboards that surface emerging risk before it becomes a finding — feeding directly into the program's daily standup.

Automated Reporting

Recurring deliverables generated from the same source-of-truth data — no parallel spreadsheets, no copy-paste drift.

Operational Insights Integration

Closes the loop with ADEPT consulting telemetry so that recurring gaps observed in the field become productized controls.

Where RMFM Sits in the ADROIT Lifecycle

RMFM is the productized output of repeated ADEPT consulting findings. The lifecycle is intentional, not accidental.

1. Field Observation

ADEPT consultants identify a recurring risk-management gap across multiple government programs.

2. Disciplined Innovation

The gap is translated into a candidate capability — vetted against compliance, auditability, and sustainability from inception.

3. Productization

The capability becomes part of RMFM, supported by operational proof rather than marketing claims.
