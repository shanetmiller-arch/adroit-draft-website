About ADROIT

Translating consulting execution into government-grade software products

ADEPT and ADROIT translate real-world consulting execution into government-grade software products. Products such as Emerging Suite of Program Monitoring Information Reporting System (PMRIS) Application or Risk Management Framework (RMFM) Flagship product are direct responses to recurring operational pain points observed across live government programs.

Together, both companies naturally harmonizing in a lifecycle where consulting execution informs insight into recurring gaps, driving disciplined innovation that becomes productized capability supported by operational proof.

Innovation is outcome-driven and production-focused, enabling rapid development with built-in security, auditability, and sustainability from inception.
