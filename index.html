About ADROIT

Translating consulting execution into government-grade software

ADROIT and ADEPT translate real-world consulting execution into government-grade software products. Products such as the Emerging Suite of Program Monitoring Information Reporting System (PMRIS) Application or Risk Management Framework (RMFM) Flagship product are direct responses to recurring operational pain points observed across live government programs.

Innovation is outcome-driven and production-focused, enabling rapid development with built-in security, auditability, and sustainability from inception. ADROIT supports a growing product portfolio, structured to allow future additions without architectural redesign.
