About ADROIT

ADROIT Innovation House (formerly ADEPT) translates real-world consulting execution into government-grade software products. Products such as the Emerging Suite of Program Monitoring Information Reporting System (PMRIS) Application or Risk Management Framework (RMFM) Flagship are direct responses to recurring operational pain points observed across live government programs.

Together, both companies naturally harmonize in a lifecycle where consulting execution informs insight into recurring gaps, driving disciplined innovation that becomes productized capability supported by operational proof. Innovation is outcome-driven and production-focused, enabling rapid development with built-in security, auditability, and sustainability from inception.
